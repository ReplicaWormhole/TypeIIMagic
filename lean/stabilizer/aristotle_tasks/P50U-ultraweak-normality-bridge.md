# P50U — ultraweak topology and normality bridge

## Context declaration

- **Infrastructure only:** develop the concrete ultraweak topology and its
  normal-functional interface for bounded operators. No manuscript claim is
  formalized directly in this package.

## Plan alignment

- **Package and lane:** `P50U`; lane `shared`.
- **Predecessors:** `P50`, integrated and locally audited. Its theorem
  `cornerTau_continuous_wot` is the input certificate to be upgraded.
- **Gate discharged:** replace the informal implication “WOT-continuous,
  therefore ultraweakly continuous and normal” by an explicit checked topology
  comparison and export a genuinely ultraweak-normal version of the P50 corner
  theorem.
- **Forbidden downstream claims:** no new traciality or faithfulness argument,
  no projection finiteness or fullness, no central type-III exclusion, no
  global trace or weight, and no Lane-H tensor-factor or factor-classification
  theorem.

## Mathematical target

Create only `AristotleCandidates/UltraweakNormality.lean`.

For a complex Hilbert space `H`, define the standard ultraweak topology on
`B(H)` as the weak topology generated by the functionals

```text
T |-> sum n, y_n (T (x_n))
```

for sequences `x_n : H`, `y_n : H ->L[ℂ] ℂ` satisfying
`Summable (fun n => ‖x_n‖ * ‖y_n‖)`. Prove the series is well-defined and each
such functional is continuous by construction. The definition must include
arbitrary absolutely summable series of matrix coefficients; it may not define
the ultraweak topology to be WOT, SOT, or the norm topology.

Develop a type copy or equivalent explicit topology API supporting:

1. conversion between ordinary bounded operators and their ultraweak-topology
   copy without changing the underlying operator;
2. continuity of the identity map from the ultraweak topology to WOT, proved
   by realizing every WOT generator `T |-> y (T x)` as a one-term ultraweak
   coefficient series;
3. a predicate `IsUltraweaklyContinuous` for complex linear functionals on
   `B(H)` and a theorem that a WOT-continuous functional is ultraweakly
   continuous, with the topology direction represented in the Lean term;
4. `cornerTau_ultraweakContinuous`, proving that P50's compressed vector
   functional is ultraweakly continuous; and
5. a new structure or predicate whose normality field is literally
   ultraweak continuity, together with an exported theorem upgrading
   `NormalFaithfulCornerTrace.normal_faithful_corner_trace` to a faithful,
   ultraweak-normal, finite tracial corner state under exactly the same P50
   hypotheses.

The existing P50 field named `normal` stores WOT continuity. Do not modify or
reinterpret that field in this package. Instead, retain it as the stronger
input certificate and expose the new ultraweak conclusion under an
unambiguous name.

## Auxiliary assumptions

Only the standard complex Hilbert-space assumptions and the explicit P50
inputs may be used. Do not add separability, finite-dimensionality, a supplied
predual, a supplied topology-comparison theorem, or a supplied normality
predicate. If the pinned Mathlib lacks supporting topology or summability
machinery, develop it in the one authorized file. The conclusion is
conditional only on P50's already explicit finite-system, represented-algebra,
commutation, and density hypotheses.

## Files and imports

Create only `AristotleCandidates/UltraweakNormality.lean`. Import
`AristotleCandidates.NormalFaithfulCornerTrace` and the required pinned Mathlib
topology/summability modules. Do not edit P50 or any unrelated file.

## Acceptance criteria

- `lake build AristotleCandidates.UltraweakNormality` succeeds.
- No `sorry`, `admit`, new axiom, `opaque` proof surrogate, or unsafe escape is
  introduced.
- The ultraweak topology is generated by arbitrary absolutely summable series
  of matrix coefficients and is not an alias for WOT, SOT, or norm topology.
- A checked declaration proves continuity of the identity map from ultraweak
  topology to WOT and a second declaration uses it to derive ultraweak
  continuity from WOT continuity.
- The exported P50 upgrade concludes literal ultraweak continuity under the
  same P50 inputs; it may not accept ultraweak continuity or normality as an
  auxiliary assumption.
- The response names all definitions and theorems used for the topology
  direction and reports any remaining gap. A merely renamed WOT field or a
  comment claiming the implication is not acceptable and must halt closeout.
