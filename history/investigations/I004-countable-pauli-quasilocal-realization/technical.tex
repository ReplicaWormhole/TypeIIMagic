\begin{abstract}
This note records the next construction layer needed to instantiate the
finite-fiber theorem of Investigations I001--I003 for the countable lattice in
the live Overleaf manuscript.  I003 gives a coherent, norm-preserving chain of
finite ordered-qubit star-algebra embeddings and compatible finite stabilizer
functionals, but its quotient is only a module direct limit.  The present
investigation fixes a concrete target: a countable configuration Hilbert space
carrying shift and phase operators, finite-site matrix subalgebras, their
norm-closed union, and a positive normalized extension of the compatible
finite functionals.  Its first Lean module now constructs the countable
configuration Hilbert space, bounded shifts and phases, and their
almost-everywhere action formulas.  This remains a construction contract, not
a claim that finite-site matrix subalgebras, a global state, or the GNS,
corner-traciality, and regional-density hypotheses are already proved.
\end{abstract}

\section{Relation to the manuscript}
\label{sec:relation_to_manuscript}

The current manuscript defines finite local algebras, identifies them by
tensoring with identities, and takes their norm-closed union in the appendix
``Infinite Stabilizer Systems.''  It then uses compatible finite density
matrices to assert a global state.  The operative displayed constructions are
the local-algebra identification, quasi-local closure, density compatibility,
and state-extension proof in
\path{writing/overleaf_prl_stabilizer_region_algebras/main.tex}, source
lines~815--999; the manuscript labels the central displayed formulas
\texttt{eq:lattice\_embedding}, \texttt{eq:local\_density\_full}, and
\texttt{eq:rho-compatibility}.

I003 checks the finite matrix and compatibility content but does not put its
module direct limit under a multiplication, involution, or $C^*$-norm.  Thus
the intended implication is not
\begin{equation}
  \text{module direct limit}
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \text{quasi-local }C^*\text{-algebra};
  \label{eq:no_automatic_colimit}
\end{equation}
which would be false without further construction.  Instead I004 must build
a concrete common ambient operator algebra first.

\section{Concrete configuration representation}
\label{sec:configuration_representation}

Let
\begin{equation}
  K:=\mathbb N\to_0\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,
  \qquad
  \cH:=L^2(K,\mathrm{count};\bbC),
  \label{eq:configuration_space}
\end{equation}
where $K$ has the discrete measurable structure and counting measure.  The
finite support in $K$ is essential: a configuration differs from the all-zero
configuration at finitely many sites, so addition by a one-site vector is a
permutation of $K$.

For $i\in\mathbb N$, write $e_i\in K$ for the configuration that is one at
$i$ and zero elsewhere.  The target local generators are
\begin{equation}
  (X_i f)(x)=f(e_i+x),
  \qquad
  (Z_i f)(x)=(-1)^{x(i)}f(x).
  \label{eq:shift_phase_generators}
\end{equation}
The first is induced by a counting-measure-preserving translation and the
second by multiplication with a bounded measurable sign function.  Lean has
now exposed these constructions in
\path{formalization_finite_fibers/FiniteFiber/CountablePauliRepresentation.lean}.
It defines the two bounded operators and proves their indicated
almost-everywhere action formulas, exact norm preservation for translations,
the identity of the neutral translation, the translation-composition identity
that the shift by $g+h$ is the composition of the shift by $g$ followed by
the shift by $h$, the involution of each $X_i$ and $Z_i$, commutation within
each generator family, same-site anticommutation, and distinct-site mixed
commutation.  These are bounded-operator statements proved from the binary
character identities through almost-everywhere equality of $L^2$
representatives.  They establish the elementary generator relations, but do
not yet supply a full phased Pauli representation or a finite/tail matrix
factorization.  At the configuration-group level, the same Lean module now
also defines the additive initial and tail filters at every cut and proves
their recomposition, their complementary coordinate-vanishing properties, and
their action on the one-site generators.  This is an algebraic prerequisite
for a finite/tail split, not yet a unitary $L^2$ factorization.

The desired finite-site conclusion is more than the generator relations.  If
$F\subset\mathbb N$ is finite, one needs a concrete unitary identification
\begin{equation}
  \cH\simeq
  \ell^2\bigl((F\to\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)\bigr)
  \mathbin{\widehat\otimes}
  \ell^2\bigl(K_{F^c}\bigr),
  \label{eq:finite_tail_factorization}
\end{equation}
under which the $F$-site Pauli algebra is
\begin{equation}
  \cA_F\simeq M_{2^{\lvert F\rvert}}(\bbC)\otimes\id.
  \label{eq:finite_local_matrix_image}
\end{equation}
Without a checked form of Eqs.~\eqref{eq:finite_tail_factorization}--
\eqref{eq:finite_local_matrix_image}, the construction has generators but
not the manuscript's finite local algebras.

\section{Concrete quasi-local algebra and state}
\label{sec:closure_state}

Once a nested initial-segment family of concrete star subalgebras
$(\cA_n)_{n\in\mathbb N}\subseteq\cB(\cH)$ is present, define its concrete
quasi-local algebra by
\begin{equation}
  \cA_{\mathrm{ql}}
  :=\overline{\bigcup_{n\in\mathbb N}\cA_n}^{\|\cdot\|}
  \subseteq\cB(\cH).
  \label{eq:concrete_quasilocal_closure}
\end{equation}
The closure must be constructed as a star subalgebra of the bounded operators;
this provides an actual $C^*$-algebra rather than a formal completion label.

For compatible finite states $\omega_n$, first define a functional on the
concrete algebraic union.  Its well-definedness uses equality of the values
along the checked finite embeddings.  To extend it to
Eq.~\eqref{eq:concrete_quasilocal_closure}, the formal proof must establish a
uniform norm bound, apply a continuous-linear extension theorem, and prove
positivity of the extension through closedness of the positive cone.  Neither
the existence nor positivity of that extension is to be inferred merely from
the linear functional on I003's module quotient.

\section{Dependency map and remaining theorem input}
\label{sec:dependency_map}

The planned checked bridge is
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    \text{finite ordered matrix chain}
    &\longrightarrow \text{concrete finite-site subalgebras of }\cB(\cH) \\
    &\longrightarrow \cA_{\mathrm{ql}} \\
    &\longrightarrow \text{positive normalized global state} \\
    &\longrightarrow \text{the generic GNS cyclic-vector layer of I002}.
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:planned_bridge}
\end{equation}
At that point, I002 supplies only the generic cyclic-vector facts.  The
regional/complementary product-orbit density, the strong extension of the
finite corner trace, and the actual local-stabilizer support construction are
still independent inputs required by the full support-corner conclusion.  In
particular, a passing build of I004's early modules must not be reported as a
proof of the manuscript theorem
\texttt{thm:finite\_projections\_exist}.

\section{Verification contract}
\label{sec:verification_contract}
