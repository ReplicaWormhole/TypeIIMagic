\begin{question}
Can the ordered finite-qubit system of I003 be realized faithfully inside a
single concrete operator algebra on a countable configuration Hilbert space,
then completed to the quasi-local $C^*$-algebra used in the manuscript?  Can
the compatible finite stabilizer functionals be extended to a positive,
normalized global state in a way that can subsequently feed the existing GNS
and support-corner layers?
\end{question}

\begin{assumption}[Fidelity rule]
The construction must distinguish three objects: the finite ordered matrix
chain, its concrete image in bounded operators, and the norm-closed
quasi-local $C^*$-algebra.  A module direct limit is not to be called an
algebraic or normed $C^*$-inductive limit.  No claim about the regional GNS
product orbit, corner traciality, or type classification may be made before
its corresponding hypothesis is implemented.
\end{assumption}

\paragraph{Approved scope.}
The investigation begins with the countable binary configuration group
\begin{equation}
  K=\mathbb N\to_0\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,
  \qquad
  \cH= L^2(K,\mathrm{count};\bbC),
  \label{eq:configuration_hilbert_space}
\end{equation}
and constructs the local shift and phase operators.  It then seeks a checked
finite/tail factorization identifying the finite-site Pauli image with the
finite matrix algebra tensored by the tail identity.  On that basis it will
form a concrete increasing union and its norm closure, and will extend the
compatible finite functionals only after proving the required boundedness and
positivity statements.

\paragraph{Deliberate boundary.}
This investigation does not assume that the countable representation alone
already supplies the local stabilizer restriction condition, the
regional/complementary GNS product-orbit density, or the strong extension of
the corner trace.  A direct-integral or type-III conclusion is also outside
its scope.  Those are separate obligations required to instantiate all
hypotheses of I002.
